# 🇿🇦 How to get licensed: South Africa

[All countries](<https://github.com/jasiek/webchirp/wiki/Home>)

_2026-09-30_

## Official procedure

1. Prepare for and register for the South African Radio League's Radio Amateur Examination (RAE); Class A is the general route, and a Class B novice route has an age limit. [Official procedure](<https://mysarl.org.za/rae-radio-amateur-examination/>)
2. Complete the practical HF assessment and pass the RAE; receive the SARL certificates. [Official procedure](<https://mysarl.org.za/rae-radio-amateur-examination/>)
3. From the October 2026 RAE onward, register on the ICASA portal and submit your own amateur licence application with the certificates, then pay ICASA's application and licence charges. [Official procedure](<https://mysarl.org.za/rae-radio-amateur-examination/>)

## Licensing authority

[Independent Communications Authority of South Africa (ICASA)](<https://www.icasa.org.za>)

## Cost

For the October 2026 RAE, SARL lists R500 for Class A or R450 for Class B. ICASA application and spectrum-licence charges are additional and paid directly to ICASA; SARL reported a R202 one-year spectrum licence for 2026/27, but the total first-time ICASA application charge was not verified. [Sources](<https://mysarl.org.za/rae-radio-amateur-examination/>) · [cdnc.heyzine.com](<https://cdnc.heyzine.com/files/uploaded/v3/245a059f0460995932ea78055093b763bbf80cfd.pdf>)

## Processing time

A current processing time could not be verified from the linked sources; ask the authority before applying.

No qualifying applicant report was found.

## Additional requirements

- The RAE is typically held twice per year, in May and October; the required practical HF assessment must also be completed. [Sources](<https://mysarl.org.za/how-do-i-get-a-license/>)

## CEPT and visiting operators

This is not a CEPT member, but it implements T/R 61-01 for eligible visiting operators. Check the licence class and host rules; residents still follow the local licensing steps above.

[CEPT membership](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/overview>) · [T/R 61-01](<https://docdb.cept.org/document/925>) · [Implementation](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/925>)
