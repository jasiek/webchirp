# 🇦🇺 How to get licensed: Australia

[All countries](<https://github.com/jasiek/webchirp/wiki/Home>)

_2026-09-30_

## Official procedure

1. Choose Foundation, Standard or Advanced and arrange the free three-part exam with an ACMA-accredited assessor; pass theory, regulations and practical assessment. [ACMA qualification route](<https://www.acma.gov.au/qualifications-operate-amateur-radio>)
2. After the assessor lodges a passing result, apply for an ACMA recognition certificate through ACMA Assist or the downloadable form and pay its fee. [Certificate application guidance](<https://www.acma.gov.au/qualifications-operate-amateur-radio>)
3. Apply separately for a call sign; once qualified and assigned one, operate under the amateur class licence rather than an individual station licence. [Call sign application](<https://www.acma.gov.au/amateur-radio-call-signs>)

## Licensing authority

[Australian Communications and Media Authority (ACMA)](<https://www.acma.gov.au/amateur-radio-licences>)

## Cost

The accredited-assessor exam is free. Current ACMA fees are AUD 48 for the recognition certificate and AUD 32 for the next-available call sign, AUD 80 in total for those two applications. [Sources](<https://www.acma.gov.au/amateur-radio-related-fees>)

## Processing time

**Official timeframe:** ACMA's usual timeframes are examination results within two weeks after assessor receipt, recognition certificate within 15 working days, and call sign within 10 working days; these are indicative and sequential steps may extend the total. [Sources](<https://www.acma.gov.au/amateur-radio-application-processing-timeframes>)

No qualifying applicant report was found.

## Additional requirements

- The exam has theory, regulatory and practical parts; theory and regulations each need at least 70%, while an assessor judges practical competence. [Sources](<https://www.acma.gov.au/qualifications-operate-amateur-radio>)
- A recognised qualification is required for a call sign, and operation must follow the conditions of the amateur class licence. [Sources](<https://www.acma.gov.au/amateur-radio-call-signs>)

## CEPT and visiting operators

This is not a CEPT member, but it implements T/R 61-01 for eligible visiting operators. Check the licence class and host rules; residents still follow the local licensing steps above.

[CEPT membership](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/overview>) · [T/R 61-01](<https://docdb.cept.org/document/925>) · [Implementation](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/925>)

## National amateur radio organization

[Wireless Institute of Australia (WIA)](<https://www.wia.org.au>)
