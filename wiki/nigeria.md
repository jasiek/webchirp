# 🇳🇬 How to get licensed: Nigeria

[All countries](<https://github.com/jasiek/webchirp/wiki/Home>)

_2026-09-30_

## Official procedure

1. Join the Nigerian Amateur Radio Society (NARS) and obtain its introduction letter and membership certificate for the amateur-station application. [Federal Ministry amateur-station checklist](<https://fmcide.gov.ng/notices/procedure-for-obtaining-applying-radio-frequency-station-licences/>)
2. Purchase the ministry application forms, complete the additional Form 0061, and submit forms and documents in duplicate for security clearance. [Forms and submission instructions](<https://fmcide.gov.ng/notices/procedure-for-obtaining-applying-radio-frequency-station-licences/>)

A complete official application sequence could not be verified; check the authority's current instructions before applying.

## Licensing authority

[Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy](<https://fmcide.gov.ng/notices/procedure-for-obtaining-applying-radio-frequency-station-licences/>)

## Cost

The Federal Ministry lists ₦10,000 to purchase the application forms; any separate amateur licence charge is unverified. [Sources](<https://fmcide.gov.ng/notices/procedure-for-obtaining-applying-radio-frequency-station-licences/>)

## Processing time

**Official timeframe:** The ministry says a station licence is assigned within one month (four weeks) after submission under normal circumstances; security clearance may affect timing. [Sources](<https://fmcide.gov.ng/notices/procedure-for-obtaining-applying-radio-frequency-station-licences/>)

No qualifying applicant report was found.

## Additional requirements

- For an amateur station, include a NARS introduction letter and membership-certificate copy, plus passport and residence permit where applicable. [Sources](<https://fmcide.gov.ng/notices/procedure-for-obtaining-applying-radio-frequency-station-licences/>)

## CEPT and visiting operators

This is not listed as a current CEPT member or T/R 61-01 implementer. CEPT visitor privileges are not established here; ask the destination authority before operating.
