# 🇵🇭 How to get licensed: Philippines

[All countries](<https://github.com/jasiek/webchirp/wiki/Home>)

_2026-09-30_

## Official procedure

1. Apply to NTC for an amateur radio operator examination admission slip and sit the scheduled exam. [Official procedure](<https://region7.ntc.gov.ph/information/regional-examination-schedule/>)
2. After passing, use NTC Form 1-03 and the Report of Rating to apply for a new Amateur Radio Operator Certificate (AT-ROC). [Official procedure](<https://ncr.ntc.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/Info/Downloadable_Forms/Form-No-NTC-1-03-APPLICATION-FOR-AMATEUR-RADIO-OPERATOR-CERTIFICATE-AMATEUR-RADIO-STATION-LICENSE.pdf>)
3. For your own station, obtain a permit to purchase/possess equipment and then apply for an Amateur Radio Station License (AT-RSL) with proof of the equipment's source. [Official procedure](<https://region7.ntc.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/6-11-REGIONAL-OFFICES-CCT-03312025.pdf>)

## Licensing authority

[National Telecommunications Commission (NTC)](<https://region7.ntc.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/6-11-REGIONAL-OFFICES-CCT-03312025.pdf>)

## Cost

NTC examination fee ₱50. The 2025 regional citizen charter lists a new operator certificate at ₱60 per year plus ₱30 documentary stamp; a new Class D or C station filing fee ₱60 and licence ₱144 per year. Permit and equipment charges may be additional. [Sources](<https://region7.ntc.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/6-11-REGIONAL-OFFICES-CCT-03312025.pdf>)

## Processing time

**Official timeframe:** NTC Regional Office Citizen Charter states three days of agency processing for a complete operator-certificate or station-licence application; exam scheduling is additional. [Sources](<https://region7.ntc.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/6-11-REGIONAL-OFFICES-CCT-03312025.pdf>)

No qualifying applicant report was found.

## Additional requirements

- For a new operator certificate: NTC Form 1-03, a valid Report of Rating, and three recent 1×1-inch ID photos. [Sources](<https://region7.ntc.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/6-11-REGIONAL-OFFICES-CCT-03312025.pdf>)
- For a new station licence: equipment purchase/possession permit, proof of equipment source, and three recent photos. [Sources](<https://region7.ntc.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/6-11-REGIONAL-OFFICES-CCT-03312025.pdf>)

## CEPT and visiting operators

This is not listed as a current CEPT member or T/R 61-01 implementer. CEPT visitor privileges are not established here; ask the destination authority before operating.
