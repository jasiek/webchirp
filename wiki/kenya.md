# 🇰🇪 Jinsi ya kupata leseni: Kenya

[Nchi zote](<https://github.com/jasiek/webchirp/wiki/Home>)

_2026-09-30_

## Utaratibu rasmi

1. Faulu mtihani unaotambuliwa wa Foundation, Intermediate au Full wa redio ya amatur unaosimamiwa na Radio Society of Kenya au msimamizi mwingine anayekubalika. [Utaratibu rasmi](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-02/Amateur%20Radio%20Licensing%20Guidelines%202023.pdf>)
2. Wasilisha fomu CA/F/FSM/02 pamoja na hati za utambulisho na cheti cha mtihani; baada ya CA kutathmini ombi, lipa ada husika za maombi na masafa ili upate leseni. [Utaratibu rasmi](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/CA/Frequency%20Spectrum%20Forms/Amateur%20Radio%20Application%20Form.pdf>)

## Mamlaka ya kutoa leseni

[Communications Authority of Kenya (CA)](<https://www.ca.go.ke/>)

## Gharama

Ratiba ya ada za masafa iliyochapishwa inaonyesha KSh 2,000 kwa kila kituo cha redio ya amatur kwa mwaka. Fomu ya sasa inahitaji ada ya maombi lakini haitaji kiasi chake; gharama ya mtihani pia haijathibitishwa. [Vyanzo](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/CA/License%20fee/Frequency%20Fee%20Schedule%20-%20Effective%201st%20July%202018%20.pdf>)

## Muda wa kushughulikia

Muda wa sasa haukuthibitishwa kutoka vyanzo vilivyounganishwa; uliza mamlaka kabla ya kuomba.

Hakuna taarifa ya mwombaji inayokidhi vigezo iliyopatikana.

## Masharti ya ziada

- Waombaji Wakenya huwasilisha kitambulisho cha taifa au pasipoti; waombaji wa kigeni huwasilisha pasipoti iliyoidhinishwa na mthibitishaji pamoja na visa ya Kenya. [Vyanzo](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/CA/Frequency%20Spectrum%20Forms/Amateur%20Radio%20Application%20Form.pdf>)
- Wasilisha uthibitisho wa kufaulu mtihani unaotambuliwa au leseni iliyopo; hakikisha maelezo ya mawasiliano na kituo yanaendelea kuwa sahihi wakati wote wa leseni. [Vyanzo](<https://www.ca.go.ke/sites/default/files/2024-02/Amateur%20Radio%20Licensing%20Guidelines%202023.pdf>)

## CEPT na waendeshaji wageni

Nchi hii haijaorodheshwa kuwa mwanachama wa sasa wa CEPT au mtekelezaji wa T/R 61-01. Haki za wageni za CEPT hazijathibitishwa hapa; uliza mamlaka ya ndani kabla ya kutuma mawimbi.

[CEPT membership](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/overview>) · [T/R 61-01](<https://docdb.cept.org/document/925>) · [Implementation](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/925>)
