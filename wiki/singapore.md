# 🇸🇬 How to get licensed: Singapore

[All countries](<https://github.com/jasiek/webchirp/wiki/Home>)

_2026-09-30_

## Official procedure

1. SARTS states that Singapore's Radio Amateur Examination is currently suspended. Obtain a qualification accepted by IMDA, such as the FCC amateur licence identified by SARTS, and confirm acceptance with IMDA. [Official procedure](<https://www.sarts.org.sg/getting-a-9v-ham-license/>)
2. Apply online through GoBusiness for the IMDA Amateur Station Licence, with evidence of the accepted qualification and equipment/antenna details. [Official procedure](<https://www.imda.gov.sg/-/media/imda/files/regulations-and-licensing/licensing/telecommunication/amateur-station/guideamateur.pdf>)

## Licensing authority

[Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA)](<https://www.imda.gov.sg/regulations-and-licensing-listing/amateur-station-licence>)

## Cost

The IMDA station licence costs S$50 for five years (Restricted) or S$100 for five years (General). The cost of an accepted foreign qualification is separate and unverified. [Sources](<https://www.imda.gov.sg/-/media/imda/files/regulations-and-licensing/licensing/telecommunication/amateur-station/amateur-handbook.pdf>)

## Processing time

A current processing time could not be verified from the linked sources; ask the authority before applying.

No qualifying applicant report was found.

## Additional requirements

- Applicant must be at least 16; an application by someone under 21 needs a parent or guardian countersignature. [Sources](<https://www.imda.gov.sg/-/media/imda/files/regulations-and-licensing/licensing/telecommunication/amateur-station/guideamateur.pdf>)
- Provide passport if foreign, amateur qualification, premises-owner approval for antenna installation, and an antenna schematic if applicable. Equipment must be IMDA approved. [Sources](<https://www.imda.gov.sg/-/media/imda/files/regulations-and-licensing/licensing/telecommunication/amateur-station/guideamateur.pdf>)

## CEPT and visiting operators
