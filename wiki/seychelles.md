# 🇸🇨 How to get licensed: Seychelles

[All countries](<https://github.com/jasiek/webchirp/wiki/Home>)

_2026-09-30_

## Official procedure

1. Pass an amateur examination and obtain a relevant radio operator's certificate; SARA describes local training and RSGB examinations. [Official procedure](<https://www.sara.sc/training>)
2. Apply to the Seychelles Communications Regulatory Authority using Schedule II of the 2026 licensing regulations; select Radio Frequency Spectrum and Amateur Radio, attach identity document and operator certificate, and supply station/equipment details. [Official procedure](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2026-01/S.I.%20No%201%202026%20-%20Communications%20%28Licensing%29%20Regulations%202026.pdf>)

## Licensing authority

[Seychelles Communications Regulatory Authority (SCRA)](<https://scra.sc/>)

## Cost

The new 2026 tariff is SCR 1,000 for a one-year amateur radio licence, or SCR 500 for a licence of up to three months; first-year fees may be prorated depending on month of issue. [Sources](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2026-01/S.I.%20No%201%202026%20-%20Communications%20%28Licensing%29%20Regulations%202026.pdf>)

## Processing time

A current processing time could not be verified from the linked sources; ask the authority before applying.

No qualifying applicant report was found.

## Additional requirements

- The operator certificate must demonstrate Article 25 operational and technical competence; the application requires a Seychellois ID copy or, for a non-Seychellois, passport copy. [Sources](<https://www.gazette.sc/sites/default/files/2026-01/S.I.%20No%201%202026%20-%20Communications%20%28Licensing%29%20Regulations%202026.pdf>)

## CEPT and visiting operators

This is not listed as a current CEPT member or T/R 61-01 implementer. CEPT visitor privileges are not established here; ask the destination authority before operating.

[CEPT membership](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/overview>) · [T/R 61-01](<https://docdb.cept.org/document/925>) · [Implementation](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/925>)
