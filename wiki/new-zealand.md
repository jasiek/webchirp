# 🇳🇿 How to get licensed: New Zealand

[All countries](<https://github.com/jasiek/webchirp/wiki/Home>)

_2026-09-30_

## Official procedure

1. Arrange the amateur operator examination with an Approved Radio Examiner (ARX) and obtain a General Amateur Operator's Certificate of Competency and callsign. [Certificate and callsign guide](<https://www.rsm.govt.nz/licensing/how-do-i/get-a-certificate-callsign>)
2. Operate under Radio Spectrum Management's General User Radio Licence for amateur radio after checking its conditions; ordinary operators do not apply for an individual spectrum licence. [Amateur radio operator rules](<https://www.rsm.govt.nz/licensing/frequencies-for-anyone/amateur-radio-operators>)

## Licensing authority

[Radio Spectrum Management (RSM)](<https://www.rsm.govt.nz/licensing/frequencies-for-anyone/amateur-radio-operators>)

## Cost

The General User Radio Licence is free. An Approved Radio Examiner may charge an independently agreed exam/callsign fee, so a fixed total cannot be verified. [Sources](<https://www.rsm.govt.nz/licensing/frequencies-for-anyone/amateur-radio-operators>)

## Processing time

A current processing time could not be verified from the linked sources; ask the authority before applying.

No qualifying applicant report was found.

## Additional requirements

- A current General Amateur Operator's Certificate of Competency and callsign are required; New Zealand certificates are available to citizens or residents. [Sources](<https://www.rsm.govt.nz/licensing/frequencies-for-anyone/amateur-radio-operators>)
- Read and follow the current General User Radio Licence conditions before transmitting. [Sources](<https://www.rsm.govt.nz/licensing/frequencies-for-anyone/amateur-radio-operators>)

## CEPT and visiting operators

This is not a CEPT member, but it implements T/R 61-01 for eligible visiting operators. Check the licence class and host rules; residents still follow the local licensing steps above.

[CEPT membership](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/overview>) · [T/R 61-01](<https://docdb.cept.org/document/925>) · [Implementation](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/925>)

## National amateur radio organization

[New Zealand Association of Radio Transmitters (NZART)](<https://www.nzart.org.nz>)
