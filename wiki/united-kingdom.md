# 🇬🇧 How to get licensed: United Kingdom

[All countries](<https://github.com/jasiek/webchirp/wiki/Home>)

_2026-09-30_

## Official procedure

1. Prepare for and book the RSGB Foundation exam, either through a club or by remote invigilation. [Official procedure](<https://rsgb.org/main/clubs-training/for-students/paying-for-your-exam/>)
2. After passing, use your RSGB candidate number in Ofcom's online licensing portal, select an available call sign and obtain the licence document before transmitting. [Official procedure](<https://www.ofcom.org.uk/__data/assets/pdf_file/0026/82637/amateur_radio_licence_guidance_for_licensees.pdf>)

## Licensing authority

[Ofcom](<https://www.ofcom.org.uk/siteassets/resources/documents/manage-your-licence/amateur/amateur_radio_licence_guidance_for_licensees.pdf>)

## Cost

Foundation exam £35.50; online Ofcom licence free (postal/email application £20 under age 75). [Sources](<https://rsgb.org/main/faq-2/exam-candidates-faq/>) · [www.ofcom.org.uk](<https://www.ofcom.org.uk/__data/assets/pdf_file/0026/82637/amateur_radio_licence_guidance_for_licensees.pdf>)

## Processing time

**Official timeframe:** Ofcom says online licence document is produced as soon as the application is complete; postal or email application may take up to 10 working days to process and invoice, then issues after payment. This is official service guidance, not a statutory deadline. [Sources](<https://www.ofcom.org.uk/__data/assets/pdf_file/0026/82637/amateur_radio_licence_guidance_for_licensees.pdf>)

**Applicant report since 2025:** In May 2025, an applicant reported waiting a week for RSGB exam results around bank holidays and later confirmed receiving the licence. Another participant estimated that RSGB confirmation can take up to two weeks; once it arrives, the Ofcom online licence is issued almost immediately. These reports do not establish a guaranteed total time. [Sources](<https://www.reddit.com/r/amateurradio/comments/1kka9zh>)

## Additional requirements

- A passed exam is required; Foundation, Intermediate and Full are different levels. Full is the level recognized for CEPT visitor privileges. [Sources](<https://rsgb.org/main/operating/licensing-novs-visitors/uk-licensing/>)

## CEPT and visiting operators

This is a CEPT member that implements T/R 61-01. A qualifying national licence may allow temporary operation in other participating places under the host rules; it does not replace a resident licence. The licence class must be eligible.

[CEPT membership](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/overview>) · [T/R 61-01](<https://docdb.cept.org/document/925>) · [Implementation](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/925>)

## National amateur radio organization

[Radio Society of Great Britain (RSGB)](<https://rsgb.org/>)
