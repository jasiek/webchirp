# 🇨🇦 How to get licensed: Canada

[All countries](<https://github.com/jasiek/webchirp/wiki/Home>)

_2026-09-30_

## Official procedure

1. Prepare for the Basic qualification exam / Préparez-vous à l'examen de la qualification de base. [Official procedure](<https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/spectrum-management-telecommunications/en/licences-and-certificates/radio-authorizations/amateur-radio-operator-certification/how-become-amateur-radio-operator-overview>)
2. Find an ISED-accredited examiner and pass the Basic exam / Trouvez un examinateur accrédité et réussissez l'examen de base. [Official procedure](<https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/spectrum-management-telecommunications/en/licences-and-certificates/radio-authorizations/amateur-radio-operator-certification/how-become-amateur-radio-operator-overview>)
3. After the examiner submits the result and gives an authorization key, create the ISED account and choose an available call sign / Après la transmission du résultat, créez un compte et choisissez un indicatif disponible. [Official procedure](<https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/spectrum-management-telecommunications/en/licences-and-certificates/radio-authorizations/amateur-radio-operator-certification/how-become-amateur-radio-operator-overview>)

## Licensing authority

[Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED), Amateur Radio Service Centre](<https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/spectrum-management-telecommunications/en/licences-and-certificates/radio-authorizations/amateur-radio-operator-certification>)

## Cost

ISED charges no exam/certificate fee; an accredited examiner may charge a variable cost-recovery fee. [Sources](<https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/spectrum-management-telecommunications/en/licences-and-certificates/radiocom-information-circulars-ric/ric-1-guide-examiners-accredited-conduct-examinations-amateur-radio-operator-certificates>)

## Processing time

**Official timeframe:** L’examinateur doit transmettre les résultats positifs dans les 10 jours ouvrables; aucun délai d’émission du certificat par ISDE n’a été vérifié. [Sources](<https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/spectrum-management-telecommunications/en/licences-and-certificates/radiocom-information-circulars-ric/ric-1-guide-examiners-accredited-conduct-examinations-amateur-radio-operator-certificates>)

**Applicant report since 2025:** A new Canadian operator said exam validation and call-sign registration appeared in ISED's database within minutes after online submission. This measures the final electronic registration phase only; their paper certificate had not arrived. [Sources](<https://www.reddit.com/r/amateurradio/comments/1s3shxu/canadian_amateur_radio_license_certificates/>)

## Additional requirements

- Basic exam has 100 questions and requires 70%; 80% grants Basic with Honours and additional HF privileges / 100 questions, réussite à 70 %, privilèges HF supplémentaires à 80 %. [Sources](<https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/spectrum-management-telecommunications/en/licences-and-certificates/radiocom-information-circulars-ric/ric-3-information-amateur-radio-service>)
- For remote examination the examiner and candidate must both be in Canada / Pour un examen à distance, les deux doivent être au Canada. [Sources](<https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/spectrum-management-telecommunications/en/licences-and-certificates/radiocom-information-circulars-ric/ric-1-guide-examiners-accredited-conduct-examinations-amateur-radio-operator-certificates>)

## CEPT and visiting operators

This is not a CEPT member, but it implements T/R 61-01 for eligible visiting operators. Check the licence class and host rules; residents still follow the local licensing steps above.

[CEPT membership](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/overview>) · [T/R 61-01](<https://docdb.cept.org/document/925>) · [Implementation](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/925>)
