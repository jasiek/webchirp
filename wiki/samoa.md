# 🇼🇸 How to get licensed: Samoa

[All countries](<https://github.com/jasiek/webchirp/wiki/Home>)

_2026-09-30_

## Official procedure

1. Obtain an amateur-radio certificate acceptable to the Office of the Regulator, unless it grants an exemption. [Official procedure](<https://www.regulator.gov.ws/images/Rules/Radio-Spectrum-Rules-2018-Eng.pdf>)
2. Complete the Office of the Regulator's Amateur application form and submit it in writing with the prescribed fee. [Official procedure](<https://www.regulator.gov.ws/index.php/telecommunications-regulation/telecommunications/application-forms>)

## Licensing authority

[Office of the Regulator](<https://www.regulator.gov.ws/images/Application_Forms/New/form_AMA01_radiocommunication_amatuer_radio.pdf>)

## Additional requirements

- An amateur station is issued only to a qualified or approved person; it cannot be used for financial gain or by an unlicensed person. [Sources](<https://www.regulator.gov.ws/images/Rules/Radio-Spectrum-Rules-2018-Eng.pdf>)
