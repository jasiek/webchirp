# 🇺🇸 How to get licensed: United States

[All countries](<https://github.com/jasiek/webchirp/wiki/Home>)

_2026-09-30_

## Official procedure

1. Register for an FCC Registration Number (FRN) in CORES before exam day. [Official procedure](<https://apps.fcc.gov/cores/>)
2. Book a Volunteer Examiner session and pass the Technician element (or higher elements if prepared). The VE team forwards results through its VEC to the FCC. [Official procedure](<https://www.arrl.org/find-an-amateur-radio-license-exam-session>)
3. Pay the FCC application fee in CORES within 10 calendar days of the application file number; transmit only after the licence and call sign appear in the FCC record. [Official procedure](<https://www.arrl.org/fcc-application-fee>)

## Licensing authority

[Federal Communications Commission (FCC)](<https://www.fcc.gov/wireless/bureau-divisions/mobility-division/amateur-radio-service>)

## Cost

For an adult using an ARRL VE exam, the 2026 session is USD 15 and the FCC application is USD 35: USD 50 total. ARRL charges candidates under 18 USD 5 for its exam; other VEC fees may differ. [Sources](<https://www.arrl.org/fcc-application-fee>) · [www.arrl.org](<https://www.arrl.org/arrl-vec-exam-fees>)

## Processing time

A current processing time could not be verified from the linked sources; ask the authority before applying.

**Applicant report since 2025:** A first Technician applicant reported passing on Saturday, finding the FCC fee request on Monday, paying on Thursday, and receiving the call-sign confirmation early Friday: about six calendar days from exam to call sign. [Sources](<https://www.reddit.com/r/amateurradio/comments/1ul79hv/passed_my_tech/>)

## Additional requirements

- Provide an FRN and identity documentation at the test session. [Sources](<https://www.arrl.org/find-an-amateur-radio-license-exam-session>)

## CEPT and visiting operators

This is not a CEPT member, but it implements T/R 61-01 for eligible visiting operators. Check the licence class and host rules; residents still follow the local licensing steps above.

[CEPT membership](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/overview>) · [T/R 61-01](<https://docdb.cept.org/document/925>) · [Implementation](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/925>)

## National amateur radio organization

[American Radio Relay League (ARRL)](<https://www.arrl.org/>)
