# 🇮🇪 How to get licensed: Ireland

[All countries](<https://github.com/jasiek/webchirp/wiki/Home>)

_2026-09-30_

## Official procedure

1. Register with the Irish Radio Transmitters Society for the HAREC examination and pass it. [Official procedure](<https://irts.ie/dnloads/IRTS_HAREC_Exam_Application_Form.pdf>)
2. Apply to ComReg through its eLicensing portal for an Amateur Station Licence, using the HAREC qualification. [Official procedure](<https://www.comreg.ie/industry/radio-spectrum/licensing/search-licence-type/radio-amateurs-2/>)

## Licensing authority

[Commission for Communications Regulation (ComReg)](<https://www.comreg.ie/industry/radio-spectrum/licensing/search-licence-type/radio-amateurs-2/>)

## Cost

IRTS lists a €50 HAREC examination fee (€25 for listed concession categories). ComReg charges €100 for a new station licence, or €30 if aged 65+ or receiving the specified disability payment/pension. Standard exam plus licence totals €150. [Sources](<https://www.comreg.ie/industry/radio-spectrum/licensing/search-licence-type/radio-amateurs-2/>)

## Processing time

**Official timeframe:** ComReg states a general target to process, validate and complete spectrum licence applications within an average of 25 working days. This is not an amateur-specific guarantee and excludes exam scheduling. [Sources](<https://www.comreg.ie/industry/radio-spectrum/licensing/>)

No qualifying applicant report was found.

## Additional requirements

- ComReg requires a passed CEPT/HAREC TR/61-02 level examination; Novice or Intermediate qualifications alone do not qualify for a new Irish Amateur Station Licence. [Sources](<https://www.comreg.ie/media/2023/05/ComReg-0945R6.pdf>)

## CEPT and visiting operators

This is a CEPT member that implements T/R 61-01. A qualifying national licence may allow temporary operation in other participating places under the host rules; it does not replace a resident licence. The licence class must be eligible.

[CEPT membership](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/overview>) · [T/R 61-01](<https://docdb.cept.org/document/925>) · [Implementation](<https://docdb.cept.org/implementation/925>)
